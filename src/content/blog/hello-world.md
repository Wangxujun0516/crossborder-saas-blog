CrossBorder SaaS is a resource for founders, product managers, and marketers who are taking Chinese SaaS products to global markets.

## What to expect

- **Product localization** — adapting UX, pricing, and compliance for Western markets
- **Growth marketing** — SEO, content marketing, and paid acquisition for B2B SaaS
- **Operations** —跨境支付, entity setup, tax compliance, and hiring abroad

## Why this matters

China's SaaS ecosystem has matured significantly in recent years. Companies like ByteDance (飞书), Alibaba (DingTalk), and dozens of emerging startups have built world-class products. Yet most struggle with international expansion — not because of product quality, but because of go-to-market blind spots.

This blog bridges that gap.

Stay tuned for deep dives into localization strategies, case studies of successful (and failed) expansions, and tactical guides for every stage of the journey.
